Questions

Good to know

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in the free Google Sheets app on Android or iPhone, or in Excel. The setup guide shows you how in three steps.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Nothing connects to your bank. You type what you plan and what you spend, so your information stays with you.

Can I use it next year?

Yes. Change the year on the Setup tab and clear your entries. The Next Year Fund tab helps you save monthly so next Christmas is paid in cash.

Which currency does it use?

Any. Amounts are plain numbers, so it works in dollars, pounds, euros, rand or anything else.

What do I get?

The planner as an .xlsx file for Google Sheets and Excel, plus a one-page Start Here guide (PDF). You can download them straight away after checkout.
