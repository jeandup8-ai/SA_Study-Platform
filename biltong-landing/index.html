What is biltong?

The snack every South African abroad misses most

Biltong is South Africa’s air-dried, vinegar-cured beef, rubbed with toasted coriander, coarse salt and cracked pepper and dried slowly in moving air. For centuries it was how meat was kept on long journeys across southern Africa. Today it is a national obsession, eaten at the rugby, on road trips and in every lunchbox.

Outside South Africa it is hard to find and expensive. Once you know how, it is simple to make for a fraction of the price.

Is home-made biltong safe?

Biltong is a raw, cured meat preserved by salt, vinegar and drying. The guide opens with clear food-safety rules and explains who should take extra care, such as pregnant women, young children and people with weakened immune systems. Follow your local food-safety guidance.
